SELECTION CRITERIA · ECSPR ART 19(2) PUBLISHED IN FULL

How we choose the companies on this platform.

These are the criteria we apply, published in full because the regulation requires it and because you should be able to judge our standard for yourself. This is not marketing copy — it is the checklist our team actually works through.

Mandatory conditions

No exceptions are made to any of these. An application that fails one is declined, whatever else is true about the company.

  1. No criminal record for the company, its board members or its beneficial owners in respect of company law, insolvency law, financial services law, money laundering, fraud or professional liability. Checked against the register of the country of establishment and of residence, not older than three months.
  2. The company is not established in a non-cooperative tax jurisdiction or in a high-risk third country under Directive (EU) 2015/849 art 9(2).
  3. The total of all crowdfunding offers by the company in the preceding 12 months, including this one, does not exceed EUR 5,000,000 — the ceiling set by ECSPR art 1(2)(c).
  4. Share capital of at least EUR 10,000, fully paid in.
  5. Articles of association that permit share transfers without notarial form and contain minority-shareholder protections.
  6. The company is not a related party of the platform within the meaning of ECSPR art 8(2) — that rule bars our own shareholders holding 20% or more, our directors and our staff, and anyone connected to them by control or close family ties.

Assessed criteria

These are scored rather than pass/fail. A strong score in one area does not compensate for a failed mandatory condition above.

  1. Financial position — annual revenue between EUR 100,000 and EUR 100M, evidenced by filed annual reports and management accounts no older than three months.
  2. Management track record — the background of the people running the company, verified independently rather than taken from a CV.
  3. Business model and market — what the company sells, to whom, and whether the market claim stands up to a named, dated source.
  4. Use of funds and plausibility of the plan — a written breakdown by amount, checked against the business plan.
  5. Governance and structure — ownership chain to natural persons, group structure, and any related-party arrangements.
  6. Legal standing and litigation — registry searches, tax-debt confirmation, and a search for material disputes.

Sectors we do not list

Regulated financial firms Passive asset holding or letting Gambling Adult content Weapons Unlicensed crypto-asset services Companies in insolvency Companies with overdue tax debt

Entities whose main activity is holding or letting assets are excluded because such a structure can amount to a collective investment undertaking, which is regulated separately and is outside what this platform is authorised to offer.

What listing does and does not mean

Listing is not a recommendation. Meeting these criteria means a company passed our admission process and its Key Investment Information Sheet passed our completeness check. It is not an opinion on whether the investment is a good one, a forecast of its performance, or any form of endorsement. We do not give investment advice. No offer on this platform is approved or reviewed by Finantsinspektsioon or any other authority — the KIIS remains the project owner's document and the project owner's responsibility under ECSPR art 23.

We tell every applicant the outcome, including when it is a refusal, and we give the reason. A refusal is recorded with its grounds. If your circumstances change — for example a further year of accounts is filed — you are welcome to apply again.
