07 / BOARDECSPR ART 25 NOTICES

A bulletin board, not an exchange

Under ECSPR article 25 Equity24.io may operate a bulletin board where investors indicate interest to buy or sell shares. It does not match orders, execute trades, or operate as a trading venue. Price discovery is between the parties.

THE BOARD DOES
  • Display investors’ notices of buying and selling interest
  • Put the two parties in contact with each other
  • Record an agreed transfer in the nominee register once both parties instruct us
THE BOARD DOES NOT
  • Match orders, pair interests, or execute trades
  • Set, confirm or guarantee any price
  • Hold client money — payments run through the licensed payment provider

Any price in a notice is the poster’s own non-binding indication and may not reflect achievable value. A counterparty may not exist when you want to sell. When two investors agree, the transfer is completed as a digital book entry in the Aureviafund nominee register.
