Research date: 2026-08-01. Prepared for the Consulting24 crowdfunding-license SEO cluster (Estonia-anchored, EUR 37,000 direct-delivery offer). All hard facts carry inline source URLs; anything not independently confirmed is marked **UNVERIFIED**.

## 0. CRITICAL REGULATOR VERIFICATION — use Finantsinspektsioon, never "FIU"

The commercial offer document (crowdfunding-docx) repeatedly names the "Estonian Financial Intelligence Unit (FIU)" as the licensing authority (Sections 1, 3, 4: "FIU-approved crowdfunding license documentation", "FIU review process: Approximately 3–6 months"). **This is wrong.**

- Estonian ECSPR crowdfunding authorizations are issued by **Finantsinspektsioon** (the Estonian Financial Supervision Authority, EFSA). Its own page "The operating licence for crowdfunding" states applications under Regulation (EU) 2020/1503 are submitted to Finantsinspektsioon, and from 18.03.2026 exclusively through its application portal (https://www.fi.ee/en/investment/applying-operating-licence/operating-licence-crowdfunding).
- Finantsinspektsioon publicly announced the two Estonian ECSPR authorizations ever granted (Estateguru 08.05.2023: https://www.fi.ee/en/news/estateguru-was-granted-operating-authorisation-provider-crowdfunding-services; Crowdestate 06.03.2023, withdrawn at own request 13.04.2026 — first-party register research R2-ecspr-register.md, verified against fi.ee).
- The Estonian FIU (Rahapesu Andmebüroo) is the AML authority that historically issued crypto/VASP licenses — the likely source of the mix-up given Consulting24's crypto-licensing background. It has no role in ECSPR authorization.

**Action:** every SEO page, sales doc and the offer docx itself must say Finantsinspektsioon. The claim "documentation has passed two rounds of regulator review" should be re-attributed to Finantsinspektsioon review rounds; as phrased ("FIU review") it is factually impossible and a compliance/credibility risk. The docx's "3–6 months review" should also be framed correctly: statutory 25 working days completeness check + 3-month decision clock on a *complete* application; realistic elapsed time 6–9 months including completeness rounds (fi.ee process, first-party research R2 verified Aug 2026).

## 2. Country profiles (home-state choice factors)

### Estonia (baseline)
- **Regulator:** Finantsinspektsioon (EFSA) — https://www.fi.ee/en/investment/applying-operating-licence/operating-licence-crowdfunding
- **State fee:** EUR 1,000 menetlustasu (first-party R2, verified live from fi.ee Aug 2026)
- **Own funds:** EUR 25,000 or professional indemnity insurance (ECSPR Art 11 standard; insurance quoted at ~EUR 1,000–1,500/month in the Consulting24 offer)
- **Timeline:** 25 working days completeness + 3-month decision on complete file; **realistic 6–9 months elapsed** (first-party R2)
- **Language:** Estonian; application exclusively via taotlus.fi.ee portal since 18.03.2026 (fi.ee)
- **Substance:** management genuinely in Estonia; salaried CEO and CFO; compliance + risk officer CVs; IT outsourceable (offer docx, consistent with Delegated Regulation (EU) 2022/2112's 18 documentation categories)
- **Reputation:** experienced but under-employed in this vertical (2 authorizations, 1 voluntary revocation processed); a well-prepared file gets senior attention (first-party R2)
- **Quirks:** ~78% of investment volume via Estonian providers is cross-border — highest shares in the EU (ESMA data via first-party R2) — the best statistic for "license here, sell everywhere"; e-Residency enables fully remote company setup (company registration 1–5 days); zero active equity CSPs = empty niche.

### Lithuania
- **Regulator:** Lietuvos bankas / Bank of Lithuania (LB) — https://www.lb.lt/en/crowdfunding-service-providers
- **State fee:** EUR 710, paid to the State Tax Inspectorate before filing (https://www.lb.lt/en/crowdfunding-service-providers; https://adamsmith.lt/en/finance-and-blockchain/crowdfunding-licence-in-lithuania/)
- **Own funds:** EUR 25,000 or insurance (ECSPR standard; LB aligns — https://crassula.io/guides/licenses/ecsp/)
- **Timeline:** 25 days completeness + 3 months statutory; **3–5 months in practice** — the fastest credible claim in the EU (https://crassula.io/guides/licenses/ecsp/)
- **Language:** Lithuanian statutory; LB is known for English-friendly fintech handling — **UNVERIFIED for CSP filings specifically**
- **Substance:** real local management expected; LB's newcomer regime is welcoming but post-license supervision is active
- **Reputation:** fastest, most fintech-industrialized NCA in the region; 14–15 CSPs authorized, **all lending/real-estate, zero equity** (first-party R2/R3, Eurocrowd 16.01.2026)
- **Quirks:** most crowded Baltic lending register = hardest local niche; Letsinvest license expanded Apr 2025 toward securities placement (first-party R3 watch item). Lithuanian crowd-investing market: EUR 278.4M / 500k+ transactions in 2024 (first-party R3).

### Latvia
- **Regulator:** Latvijas Banka (absorbed FKTK/FCMC in 2023) — https://www.bank.lv/en/operational-areas/licensing/crowdfunding-service-providers
- **State fee:** EUR 2,500 on filing; **plus EUR 4,000/year + up to 1.4% of gross revenue (cap EUR 100,000/year) ongoing supervision fee** (bank.lv licensing page, retrieved Aug 2026)
- **Own funds:** EUR 25,000 or insurance (ECSPR standard)
- **Timeline:** 25 business days completeness + 3-month examination, extendable (bank.lv); in practice Latvijas Banka issued 4 new CSP licenses Jul 2025–Mar 2026 — currently the fastest-issuing Baltic NCA by throughput (first-party R2)
- **Language:** Latvian; free pre-licensing consultations offered, fintech-courting posture (https://www.bank.lv/en/services/services-for-fintechs)
- **Substance:** local governance expected; pragmatic scale-appropriate approach
- **Reputation:** pragmatic, supportive, issuing steadily — but the recurring EUR 4k + 1.4%-of-revenue levy is a real cost Estonia does not charge
- **Quirks:** 7 CSPs by Aug 2026, only one equity (CrowdedHero, ~EUR 600k raised in 3 years — first-party R3): proof the license alone doesn't make a market.

### Netherlands
- **Regulator:** Autoriteit Financiële Markten (AFM) — https://www.afm.nl/nl-nl/sector/crowdfundingplatformen/vereisten-en-vergunning/vergunning-aanvragen
- **State fee:** **billed hourly — EUR 200/hour capped at EUR 75,000** (Ministry of Finance tariff, AFM application page retrieved Aug 2026). The sharpest cost contrast with Estonia's flat EUR 1,000 anywhere in the EU.
- **Own funds:** EUR 25,000 or insurance (ECSPR standard)
- **Timeline:** 25 working days completeness + 3-month decision (afm.nl); practice figure **UNVERIFIED** (expect longer for novel models)
- **Language:** Dutch process; English documents commonly accepted — **UNVERIFIED**
- **Substance:** meaningful Dutch presence expected; AFM assesses trustworthiness/suitability of management and 20%+ shareholders (afm.nl)
- **Reputation:** strict, thorough, expensive; mature market (20 CSPs, 01.06.2026 register)
- **Quirks:** application form only obtainable by emailing CSPvergunningaanvraag@afm.nl; hourly billing means a weak first filing literally costs money.

### France
- **Regulator:** Autorité des marchés financiers (AMF), with ACPR opinion for loan-facilitating platforms (https://www.amf-france.org/en/professionals/fintech/my-relations-amf/crowdfunding-service-provider-csp/operating-crowdfunding-service-provider-france; https://acpr.banque-france.fr/fr/professionnels/lacpr-vous-accompagne/banque/creer-ma-societe/mes-procedures/prestataire-de-services-de-financement-participatif)
- **State fee:** **EUR 0 — AMF instruction of the PSFP dossier is free**; but market-standard EUR 20,000–50,000 in consulting/compliance spend (https://www.capsens.eu/blog/combien-coute-lobtention-de-lagrement-psfp-aupres-de-lamf)
- **Own funds:** EUR 25,000 or insurance (ECSPR standard)
- **Timeline:** 25 business days + 3 months statutory; **6–9 months at the AMF in practice** (https://www.capsens.eu/en/blog/comment-obtenir-lagrement-psfp-aupres-de-lamf-roadmap-2026)
- **Language:** French dossier (agrément PSFP)
- **Substance:** genuine French establishment and French-speaking compliance function expected
- **Reputation:** rigorous, document-heavy, slow; largest host state (52 CSPs) with a saturated domestic market concentrated in real-estate/renewables — and 2025's WiSEED judicial restructuring as the cautionary tale (first-party R4)
- **Quirks:** dual AMF/ACPR track for lending models adds coordination overhead; French market data published annually by AMF (Dec 2025 "Chiffres clés 2024 des PSFP": https://www.amf-france.org/sites/institutionnel/files/private/2025-12/publication-fra-psfp-2024-anonymisee-revu-ngg-dir-com-sg-clean.pdf).

### Germany
- **Regulator:** BaFin (Bundesanstalt für Finanzdienstleistungsaufsicht) — https://www.bafin.de/DE/unternehmen-maerkte/erlaubnis-registrierung/fintech/schwarmfinanzierung/schwarmfinanzierung_node.html
- **State fee:** fee-based per FinDAGebV (BaFin fees regulation); exact ECSP authorization amount **UNVERIFIED** (general BaFin authorization fees run EUR 2,000–20,000+ and are charged even on withdrawal/refusal — https://www.kronsteyn.law/en/overview-requirements-procedures-german-banking-licence-bafin)
- **Own funds:** EUR 25,000 or insurance (ECSPR standard)
- **Timeline:** 3-month statutory clock on complete file; BaFin authorization procedures generally run **6–18 months in practice** (kronsteyn.law; CSP-specific practice figure **UNVERIFIED**)
- **Language:** German
- **Substance:** full German governance expected; formalistic documentation culture
- **Reputation:** slow, formalistic, costly counsel — reflected in the register: only 6 ECSPR CSPs in the EU's largest economy, because most German platforms deliberately stayed under the national VermAnlG/§34f regime (Companisto — first-party R4; ECCL 2025: Germany is the only major EU market largely outside ECSPR)
- **Quirks:** OneCrowd/Seedmatch insolvency 27.07.2026 (first-party R4) leaves Germany with no profitable passportable equity platform — Germany is a passport-INTO target, not a licensing seat.

### Spain
- **Regulator:** CNMV (Comisión Nacional del Mercado de Valores) — https://cnmv.es/portal/gpage.aspx?id=RTS_ITS_Financion_Participativa&lang=en
- **State fee:** CNMV authorization tasa exists under Spanish fee law; current amount **UNVERIFIED** (low four figures expected)
- **Own funds:** EUR 25,000 or insurance (ECSPR standard)
- **Timeline:** 25 wd + 3 months statutory; CNMV practice for the ECSPR transition wave ran longer — **UNVERIFIED** precise figure
- **Language:** Spanish (PSFP — proveedor de servicios de financiación participativa; implemented via Ley Crea y Crece 18/2022)
- **Substance:** Spanish establishment and governance
- **Reputation:** experienced and first-moving — CNMV issued the EU's very first ECSPR authorization (Crowdcube Europe SL, April 2022 — first-party R4); ~26–27 registered providers (Eurocrowd; https://crowdinform.com/en/articles/spanish-crowdfunding-market-report-2026-trends-platforms-investor-opportunities)
- **Quirks:** investor-pays fee models (Startupxplore 6% + 20% carry) demonstrably underperform there — useful narrative material, not a licensing reason.

### Italy
- **Regulator:** dual — **Consob** authorizes specialized platforms and investment firms *after consulting* **Banca d'Italia**; Banca d'Italia authorizes banks/payment institutions/EMIs/financial intermediaries as CSPs after consulting Consob (https://www.consob.it/web/consob-and-its-activities/crowdfunding-service-providers; https://www.bancaditalia.it/compiti/vigilanza/accesso-mercato/servizi-crowdfunding/index.html?com.dotmarketing.htmlpage.language=1)
- **State fee:** Consob annual supervisory contributions apply; application-stage fee **UNVERIFIED**
- **Own funds:** EUR 25,000 or insurance (ECSPR standard)
- **Timeline:** 3-month statutory; dual-authority consultation adds friction in practice — **UNVERIFIED** precise figure
- **Language:** Italian
- **Substance:** Italian establishment; two supervisors to keep happy
- **Reputation:** deep domestic tradition (Italy invented regulated equity crowdfunding in 2013); second-largest host (41 CSPs) but a bureaucratic dual-track regime
- **Quirks:** authorized providers must start operations within 18 months or lose the license (ECSPR Art 17, emphasized by Consob — consob.it).

### Ireland
- **Regulator:** Central Bank of Ireland (CBI) — https://www.centralbank.ie/regulation/industry-market-sectors/crowdfunding-service-providers/authorisation-process
- **State fee:** **no application fee**; ongoing Industry Funding Levy applies (first levied on the CSP sector in 2024 — https://www.centralbank.ie/docs/default-source/regulation/how-we-regulate/fees-levies/industry-funding-levy/guidance/funding-strategy-and-guide-to-the-2025-industry-funding-regulations.pdf)
- **Own funds:** EUR 25,000 or insurance (ECSPR standard)
- **Timeline:** statutory 3 months on complete file; CBI process includes a mandatory preliminary meeting plus Fitness & Probity Individual Questionnaires for all PCF roles — in practice among the longer EU processes, **UNVERIFIED** precise figure (https://www.centralbank.ie/docs/default-source/regulation/industry-market-sectors/crowdfunding-service-providers/authorisation-process/guidance-note-for-authorisation-as-a-crowdfunding-service-provider.pdf)
- **Language:** English — the only large English-language ECSPR seat
- **Substance:** heavy — CBI expects "mind and management" in Ireland, local PCF roles, real Irish operations (McCann FitzGerald: https://www.mccannfitzgerald.com/knowledge/financial-services-regulation/ireland-as-a-location-for-crowdfunding-service-providers-2023)
- **Reputation:** credible, rigorous, thorough; 7 CSPs including Republic Europe/Seedrs (first-party R4)
- **Quirks:** 12.5% corporate tax and post-Brexit prestige attract UK platforms; total cost of Irish substance (salaries, premises, levies) dwarfs any fee saving.

### Poland
- **Regulator:** KNF (Komisja Nadzoru Finansowego) — https://www.knf.gov.pl/podmioty/dostawcy_uslug_finansowania_spolecznosciowego
- **State fee:** up to the PLN equivalent of **EUR 4,500**; annual supervision fee up to 0.5% of average crowdfunding revenue, minimum EUR 750 equivalent (Polish crowdfunding act of 7 July 2022 — https://dudkowiak.pl/dzialalnosc-regulowana/crowdfunding/)
- **Own funds:** EUR 25,000 or insurance (ECSPR standard)
- **Timeline:** 3-month statutory; in practice longer — KNF routinely issues successive completion requests (https://www.dudkowiak.pl/blog/dzialalnosc-crowdfundingowa-a-zezwolenie-knf.html); precise practice figure **UNVERIFIED**
- **Language:** Polish
- **Substance:** Polish establishment and governance
- **Reputation:** strict, slow, formalistic — visible in the count: 4 CSPs for a 38M-person economy
- **Quirks:** Polish law restricts public promotion of acquiring shares in a sp. z o.o. (limited liability company) — equity crowdfunding effectively limited to S.A./P.S.A. joint-stock forms (Polish crowdfunding act; https://millercanfield.pl/ustawa-o-finansowaniu-spolecznosciowym-dla-przedsiewziec-gospodarczych-crowfunding/). Polish founders are natural license-shoppers abroad.

### Czech Republic
- **Regulator:** Česká národní banka (ČNB) — https://www.cnb.cz/en/supervision-financial-market/conduct-of-supervision/licensing-and-approval-proceedings/licensing-and-approval-proceedings-crowdfunding-service-providers/

- **Quirks:** fee-wise the only near-peer to Estonia/Lithuania; but no cross-border success story has emerged from Prague yet.

## 3. (a) Ranked shortlist — best Estonia-vs-X comparison pages to build

Ranking = founder search interest x clarity of contrast with Estonia. Slug pattern: `/estonia-vs-{x}-crowdfunding-license/` (mirrors the proven `/{a}-vs-{b}-crypto-license/` pattern).

1. **Estonia vs Lithuania** — The Baltic default (14 CSPs, EUR 710 fee, 3–5 months) vs the empty-register seat: Lithuania is where the lending crowd went; Estonia is where an equity or contrarian founder gets EUR 1,000 filing, senior regulator attention and zero domestic competition. Highest search interest — this audience already compares EE vs LT from the crypto era.
2. **Estonia vs Latvia** — One-off EUR 1,000 vs EUR 2,500 + EUR 4,000/year + up to 1.4% of gross revenue forever: Estonia wins total cost of ownership even though Latvijas Banka currently issues fastest in the Baltics.

4. **Estonia vs Netherlands** — The sharpest single number in the EU: AFM bills the application at EUR 200/hour up to EUR 75,000 vs Estonia's flat EUR 1,000 — a 75x worst-case difference on the state fee line alone.
5. **Estonia vs Germany** — License where it's fast, sell where the money is: BaFin's 6–18-month culture and 6-CSP register vs Estonian authorization + free Art 18 passport into the EU's largest, incumbent-free (post-OneCrowd-insolvency) market.
6. **Estonia vs France** — Don't domicile in the biggest market: 52 CSPs, French-only dossiers, 6–9 months at the AMF and a WiSEED-scarred sector vs passporting French deal flow from a EUR 1,000 Estonian seat.
7. **Estonia vs Poland** — 4 CSPs for 38 million people, KNF completion-request loops, and the sp. z o.o. equity-promotion ban vs Estonia's fully digital OÜ mechanics — aimed squarely at Polish founders already licensing abroad.
8. **Estonia vs Spain** — Crowdcube's home regulator (first ECSPR license ever) but Spanish-language filings and a 26-platform register vs being platform #2 in Estonia with the regulator's full attention.
9. **Estonia vs Czech Republic** — The only fee near-peer (CZK 20,000): decision comes down to language, e-Residency remote setup, cross-border DNA (78% of Estonian platform volume is cross-border) and a documentation package already proven through two Finantsinspektsioon review rounds.
10. **Estonia vs Italy** — One regulator vs two: Consob + Banca d'Italia consultation loops and the 18-month use-it-or-lose-it rule vs a single-NCA process — plus Italy as passport target #2 by platform count.
