COMPLAINTS PROCEDURE · ECSPR ART 7 NO ACCOUNT NEEDED

How to complain, and what we do about it.

If something we did was wrong, we want to hear it. A complaint tells us a control failed — it is information, not a nuisance, and we treat it that way.

COST OF MAKING A COMPLAINT
EUR 0

Free of charge, always. No fee, no deposit, no cost of any kind — and you do not need an account to complain.

We write down what you tell us and confirm it back to you.

We accept complaints in Estonian and English, and in the official language of any member state where we actively provide services following a notification under ECSPR art 18. A complaint that reaches any of our staff by any route is forwarded to the complaints mailbox the same business day.

What to tell us

Use whatever form you like — a complaint is not rejected for being in the wrong format. It helps if you include:

  1. Your name, and whether you are an investor, a prospective investor, a project owner or a prospective project owner.
  2. How to reach you, and the language you would prefer we reply in.
  3. Your account reference, if you have one.
  4. The offer or project owner involved, if the complaint is about a specific one.
  5. The dates of what happened.
  6. What happened, and what you think went wrong.
  7. What loss or harm you say it caused.
  8. What you would like us to do about it.
  9. Any documents that support what you describe.

If something is missing we ask you for it. Our deadlines below do not pause for minor gaps — only where the missing information is genuinely essential, and we tell you when that happens and why.

What happens next

01
We register your complaint
It goes into our complaints register with a unique reference.
1 business day
02
We acknowledge it in writing
You get the reference, the name and contact details of the person handling it, and the deadline for our answer.
5 business days
03
Someone not involved investigates
The handler is never a person whose own work is the subject of the complaint. They review the account file, system logs, the KIIS version you were shown, the warnings displayed and the consents recorded.
Ongoing
04
We give you a reasoned final answer
In plain language, addressing each point you raised, stating the outcome, the reasons, and any redress with how it was calculated. If we cannot answer within 15 business days we send you an interim reply explaining why and when you will hear from us.
15 business days
05
We pay any redress
Within 10 business days of your acceptance.
10 business days
06
We fix the cause
The root cause is recorded. Where it affected other people, we identify and put them right too — whether or not they complained.
10 business days

In every case you receive a final answer no later than 35 business days from when your complaint reached us.

Full contact details for each of these are included in our final answer to you.

Things we will never do

Our commitments to you. We will never charge you for making a complaint, in any form. We will never require a particular form as a condition of dealing with it. We will never offer redress on condition that you keep it confidential or that you do not contact a supervisory authority. We will never treat you worse — including by restricting your access to the platform — because you complained. And we will never close a complaint without a written, reasoned answer.

Complaints about a project owner

If your complaint is about a company that raised money here rather than about us, tell us anyway. We pass it to the project owner, we record it, and we look at whether it points to a failure in our own due diligence or in the completeness check we ran on the KIIS. You can also complain to the project owner directly — their contact details are in the KIIS.

Suspected fraud

We will never ask you to transfer money to a project owner directly, or to any account other than the one shown on the platform. If anyone asks you to — even claiming to act for us — do not pay. Report it immediately to info@equity24.io.
