## Architecture

- Entity home page (/about): one canonical page carrying legal name, brand name, LEI, Finantsinspektsioon licence number + grant date, founding date, HQ, founders — the exact same one-sentence boilerplate reused verbatim on LinkedIn, Crunchbase, press releases, Wikidata description and PR footers. Entity-consistency across sources is the #1 knowledge-graph inclusion signal in 2025-26 practice; contradictions (different descriptions/founding dates) suppress entity confidence.
- Schema.org layer (JSON-LD, server-rendered): sitewide Organization + FinancialService (with sameAs -> Wikidata Q-ID, Crunchbase, LinkedIn, FI register URL, ESMA register URL, LEI via GLEIF); per-offer pages Offer + InvestmentOrDeposit (amount, currency, minimum EUR investment) — note InvestmentOrDeposit has no interestRate for equity, use description + amount; FAQPage on all guide/FAQ pages; Article (author, datePublished, dateModified) on guides; BreadcrumbList sitewide; Dataset schema on /statistics and each quarterly report (with distribution CSV/JSON URLs). Avoid marking fabricated review/aggregateRating data — financial vertical gets manual-action scrutiny.
- Crawler access before cleverness: robots.txt explicitly allows GPTBot, OAI-SearchBot, ClaudeBot, Claude-SearchBot, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended, CCBot, Amazonbot, Applebot-Extended; all money pages are static/SSR HTML (no JS-required content); XML sitemap with lastmod; fast TTFB. This is the prerequisite every 2026 GEO study ranks above llms.txt.
- llms.txt + llms-full.txt: implement as 1-hour hygiene, not strategy — 2026 state of play: no major consumer engine (ChatGPT search, Perplexity, Gemini, AI Overviews, Claude) has confirmed consuming it; Google's Mueller/Illyes confirmed non-use (Sep 2025); ~97% of files get no bot hits. Spec: markdown index at /llms.txt with H1 platform name, one-line summary, then sections (## Docs: /about, /licence, /fees, /how-it-works, /statistics, /guides/*) each with one-line descriptions; /llms-full.txt concatenating full markdown of those pages. Costs nothing, covered if adoption flips.
- Answer-hub content pattern: every guide page opens with a 40-60 word direct answer under an H1 phrased as the query; H2s are literal follow-up questions; 2-3 quantified stats per ~300 words; comparison data in real HTML tables (LLMs lift tables); visible dateModified refreshed at least quarterly; named human author with credentials (E-E-A-T for YMYL financial content).
- /statistics — live public metrics page: total raised, campaigns funded, funding success rate, median round size, investor count, median ticket; monthly dated snapshots kept at stable URLs (LLMs cite 'as of' data); machine-readable JSON + CSV endpoints; Dataset schema. Equity platforms have no default-rate to publish, so publish valuation/follow-on/exit tracking instead.
- Quarterly 'Baltic Equity Crowdfunding Market Report' (permanent URL series, /reports/2027-q1 etc.): compiled from ESMA crowdfunding register, FI/Bank of Lithuania/FIN-FSA registers, and platform self-reported data; each edition leads with 3-5 one-sentence citable stats; distributed via press release + direct pitch to Sifted/EU-Startups/ArcticStartup/Äripäev; PDF + HTML + CSV. This is the original-data moat — Perplexity and Claude over-index on primary data sources.
- Comparison layer on own domain: /compare/equity-crowdfunding-platforms-estonia (fair table incl. Funderbeam, Fundwise, Republic Europe, Invesdor: licence status, instruments, investor fees, minimums, secondary market), /compare/vs-funderbeam, /compare/vs-republic-europe, /fees. Factual, sourced, updated quarterly — 'alternatives/vs' pages are among the most-quoted commercial page types in AI answers.
- Regulatory trust page (/licence, /et/litsents): licence number, grant date, competent authority, deep links to the FI public register entry and the ESMA register of crowdfunding service providers, list of passported member states, LEI. Regulator registers are the citable ground truth LLMs use to verify 'is X legitimate/licensed'.
- Public KIIS repository: every key investment information sheet published as indexed HTML + PDF at stable URLs (not behind login) — primary offer documents LLMs and journalists can cite; doubles as art 23 compliance surface.
- Estonian-language mirror (/et/*): natively written, not machine-translated; hreflang en/et; Estonian schema descriptions; separate Estonian press boilerplate. Estonian AI answers currently source financer.ee, rahafoorum.ee, Postimees — thin competition, winnable fast.
- Wikidata + Wikipedia: create Wikidata item pre-launch (instance of: crowdfunding platform/business; industry; HQ Tallinn; founders; official website; LEI; Crunchbase ID; FI register ref). Wikipedia: do NOT create a company article at launch (fails WP:NCORP); instead, after independent press exists, propose additions to the actively-maintained 'Crowdfunding in Estonia' article (26 platforms listed, equity section thin, last edited Nov 2025) via talk page/COI-compliant edit requests.
- Newsroom (/press): datelined releases for licence grant, first funded round, each report edition; consistent boilerplate paragraph; downloadable logo/fact-sheet. Feeds the Forbes/Business Insider/trade-press tier that ChatGPT over-cites.
- AI-answer monitoring: monthly scripted prompt panel (the 18 keywords above) run across ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini, AI Overviews in en+et; log mention/citation/sentiment; baseline before launch. Tooling: Profound/Otterly.ai or a manual spreadsheet — measurement is what turns AEO from faith into iteration.

## Recommendations

- 1. (Pre-licence, low effort, do now) Entity foundation: register LEI, create Wikidata item, Crunchbase profile, LinkedIn company page, /about entity page, Organization+FinancialService JSON-LD, and lock ONE boilerplate sentence used identically everywhere. 2-3 days of work; everything downstream compounds on it.
- 2. (At authorisation, near-zero effort, highest authority) Register-driven visibility: ensure ESMA register and FI register entries carry the exact brand name and platform URL (these fields propagate into eurocrowd.org/platforms-under-ecspr/ automatically, no membership required); publish /licence and /et/litsents the same week. Verify against the live ESMA register whether any Estonian ECSPR equity platform exists at grant date — Eurocrowd currently shows only real-estate/lending Estonian entries (Crowdestate, Estateguru) — and if the gap holds, make 'first ECSPR-authorised equity crowdfunding platform in Estonia' the lead claim in every asset; a verifiable superlative is the strongest LLM-quotable fact a new brand can own.
- 3. (Launch month, low-medium effort) Directory blitz — get listed with complete profiles on: crowdinform.com (has per-platform review pages + Estonia country page), thecrowdspace.com (1000+ platform directory), p2pmarketdata.com (top-90, monthly-updated), findcrowdfunding.com (country-EE page), sourceforge.net crowdfunding/europe category (surprisingly cited for 'best in Europe'), plus Eurocrowd membership (optional, adds a second listing surface). These directory pages ARE the current AI answers for the P1 commercial queries.
- 4. (Launch quarter, medium effort, paid in part) Estonian affiliate/media placements: financer.ee 'Ühisrahastus 2026' list, rahafoorum.ee, laenatargalt.ee, laenu-kalkulaator.ee — all lending-heavy, an equity platform is novel content for them; pitch Äripäev (Investor Toomas ecosystem), Postimees majandus, and the Rahareede/investing-podcast circuit for earned coverage. Estonian-language AI answers have very few sources, so 3-4 placements can flip them.
- 5. (Launch quarter, medium effort) English 'best of' list outreach: crowdsourcingweek.com, technews180.com, EU-Startups, Sifted, ArcticStartup, jeangalea.com-style independent reviewers — hook = EUR 0 investor fees + register-based ownership + first-mover licence claim. ChatGPT's citation diet is Wikipedia/Reddit/Forbes/trade-press; being in 3-5 refreshed listicles matters more than any on-site tweak.
- 6. (From month 1, ongoing, medium effort) Reddit + forum presence: Reddit is the #1 cited domain across all five engines (~40% aggregate share; 46.7% on Perplexity). Founder-transparent participation (flair, no astroturfing) in r/eupersonalfinance, r/EuropeFIRE, r/eesti, plus rahafoorum.ee and Estonian investing Facebook groups; one launch AMA; answer 'how to invest in Estonian startups' threads with disclosure. Astroturfing risk: bans + brand damage — use named founder accounts only.
- 7. (Quarter 2, high effort, biggest moat) Ship /statistics (machine-readable, monthly snapshots) and the first quarterly Baltic Equity Crowdfunding Market Report; pitch every edition to trade press. Becoming the primary data source for Baltic ECSPR equity crowdfunding is the only durable citation advantage a small platform can build.
- 8. (Quarter 2, medium effort) Build the comparison layer: /compare/equity-crowdfunding-platforms-estonia, /compare/vs-funderbeam, /compare/vs-republic-europe, /fees — factual HTML tables, quarterly refresh with visible dateModified.
- 9. (Ongoing, low effort each) Answer-hub guides in en+et per the keyword map: definition-first paragraphs, FAQPage schema, named authors, 2-3 stats per 300 words; publish KIIS documents as indexed HTML.
- 10. (After 12+ months of independent coverage, low effort) Wikipedia: COI-compliant edit request adding the platform (with press citations) to the equity section of 'Crowdfunding in Estonia'; only attempt a standalone article once WP:NCORP-grade coverage exists. Premature creation risks deletion + reputational trace.
- 11. (1 hour, anytime) Add llms.txt/llms-full.txt and the AI-crawler robots.txt allowlist. Evidence says llms.txt is not consumed by major engines (Google confirmed non-use, Sep 2025) — file it as hygiene, never report it as the AEO strategy.
- 12. (Monthly, low effort) Run the 18-query monitoring panel across ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini, AI Overviews in both languages; track mention/citation share and adjust outreach to whichever sources each engine actually cited that month.
