## Architecture

- Two money-page trunks matching the two-sided marketplace: /investorile (targets: ettevõtetesse investeerimine, ühisrahastus, idufirmadesse investeerimine reframed) and /ettevottele (targets: kapitali kaasamine, ühisrahastus ettevõttele, investorite kaasamine) — the raiser side is the SEO white space: its SERPs contain only advisory firms, no platforms.
- /projektid — live offers index; each offer page ships with the KIIS as an indexable HTML summary (not just PDF), company financials, and FAQ schema. These become the long-tail 'invest in [company]' pages and the proof layer for everything else.
- /teadmiskeskus (learn hub) with 5 clusters deliberately mapped onto ECSPR-mandated investor-education/disclosure content so compliance work IS content work: (1) Ühisrahastuse ABC — mis on ühisrahastus, osaluspõhine vs laenupõhine, ECSPR määrus/tegevusluba, KIIS lahtiseletatuna, 4-päevane järelemõtlemisaeg, riskihoiatused (art 19/21); (2) Maksud — flagship 'Ühisrahastus ja investeerimiskonto' (1 July 2025 law change — verified, and Crowdestate, the only site that covered it, is gone), tulude deklareerimine (seasonal), dividendide maksustamine 2026, investeerimine ettevõtte alt, OÜ osa müügi maksustamine; (3) OÜ osade mehaanika — osa müük ilma notarita, põhikirja vorminõude loobumine, äriregister kui omandiõiguse allikas, cap-table; this cluster is the product differentiator in content form; (4) Riskid ja investorikaitse — riskid, hajutamine, knowledge-test topics as public articles; (5) Kuhu investeerida — year-versioned pillar + amount spokes (1000/10 000/50 000 eurot).
- /vordlus — comparison layer: Funderbeam, Fundwise (dormant — verified), Crowdestate (licence revoked Apr 2026 — verified, newsjack now), Estateguru, Mintos, plus concept comparisons (äriinglid vs ühisrahastus, ärilaen vs omakapital, aktsiad vs ühisrahastus, toetus vs investor). One template, honest tone, updated dates.
- /kalkulaatorid — tool layer as the primary link-magnet strategy for a new domain: dividendikalkulaator (autocomplete-verified demand), 10%-netovara kahjusimulaator (the mandatory ECSPR simulation exposed as a public tool), ärilaenu-vs-omakapitali kulu kalkulaator (raiser), ettevõtte väärtuse kalkulaator (raiser).
- /sonastik — Estonian-first glossary (KIIS/põhiteabedokument, järelemõtlemisaeg, järelturg/bulletin board, mitteküps investor, osalus, osakapital) — no one owns Estonian ECSPR terminology yet.
- Trust/regulatory pages in main nav, not footer: 'Kuidas meid reguleeritakse' (link to FI register entry), 'Mis juhtub, kui platvorm kaob' (registry-title insolvency story), 'Hinnakiri' (public €1000 + 6%, €0 investor fees — competitors don't publish this clearly).
- i18n: build on subfolders (/et default, later /fi /lv /lt /es per art 18 passporting) with hreflang from day one; also an /en mirror of investor pages — autocomplete shows Estonians appending 'reddit' and searching platform names in English, and EE has a large English-speaking investor community.
- Seasonal refresh calendar baked into the architecture: tax cluster refreshed every January (deklareerimine peaks Feb–Apr), 'kuhu investeerida [year]' and 'dividendide tulumaks [year]' re-versioned annually with stable URLs.

- Small-language-market physics: EE volumes are tiny (most raiser terms show zero autocomplete depth), so (a) rankings are won with E-E-A-T signals + a handful of authority links, not content volume; (b) calculators/tools out-earn articles for links; (c) brand search becomes your biggest 'keyword' fast — invest in PR and podcast appearances over long-tail content farming; (d) expect 30–40 truly rankable non-brand terms, which the map above covers.
- Raiser-side is the strategic SEO priority even though volumes are lower: deal flow is the marketplace bottleneck, 'kapitali kaasamine' SERP has zero platforms (only Sorainen/Hoovi/Berghal advisory content — verified Aug 2026), and one ranked raiser page pays back ~€10k–50k per closed listing (1000€ + 6% of 150–800k).
- Content compliance guardrail: every investor-side page doubles as marketing communication under ECSPR art 27 (fair, clear, not misleading; risk warnings) — bake the standard risk-warning component and 'past performance' rules into the CMS template now, since FI reviews marketing of licence holders; this also conveniently produces the E-E-A-T signals Google rewards in finance (YMYL).
- Do not chase: 'ärilaen' head terms (lender-owned, wrong intent — intercept via the alternative/calculator page only), 'investeerimine algajale' head term in year 1 (bank-owned), 'hooandja' (donation intent), generic 'p2p laenud' (wrong product and reputationally toxic post-Estateguru — verified defaults/losses coverage).
