## Architecture

- Domain: one brandable gTLD (.com preferred, .eu acceptable fallback) — NOT .ee, which handicaps later FI/LV/LT/ES passporting and international-investor trust. Redirect brand.ee to the .com. Never split languages across domains.
- i18n via subdirectories: /et/ (Estonian) and /en/ (English) at launch; /en/ is x-default since international investors are the growth audience while /et/ serves the home market and regulator optics. Reserve /fi/, /lv/, /lt/, /es/ for passporting phases. Full hreflang matrix (self-referencing + x-default) in XML sitemaps.
- Top-level EN structure: /en/ (home) | /en/invest (live deal hub) | /en/deals/{company-slug} (live offer incl. HTML KIIS) | /en/funded/{company-slug} (permanent post-raise archive) | /en/raise (project owners) | /en/pricing | /en/how-it-works | /en/bulletin-board (art 25 secondary listings) | /en/learn/{guide} (academy) | /en/regulation/{topic} | /en/compare/{competitor}-alternative | /en/sectors/{sector} | /en/invest/{country} | /en/statistics | /en/about (team, licence, contacts) | /en/blog/{post}.
- Two-audience split is hard-coded in IA: investor funnel (/invest + /learn) and raiser funnel (/raise + raiser guides) with separate nav paths and separate conversion goals; never mix CTAs on one page.
- Programmatic SEO asset #1 — funded-deals archive: every closed raise keeps a permanent, indexable page (amount raised, number of investors, valuation, sector, outcome updates). Ranks for '{company} crowdfunding/review/investors' brand queries indefinitely and is the platform's compounding organic moat; also the strongest E-E-A-T 'experience' evidence Google can crawl.
- Programmatic SEO asset #2 — sector hubs (/en/sectors/manufacturing, /software, /green-energy, /consumer-brands...): auto-populated from deal tags + a 300-word editorial intro + sector funding stats. Publish only when >=2 deals exist; keep drafts noindexed to avoid thin-content penalties at launch.
- Programmatic SEO asset #3 — country pages (/en/invest/estonia now; /finland, /latvia, /lithuania activated at each art 18 passport notification): local market stats, tax notes, local-language cluster added on activation (fi: joukkorahastus terms; lv: pūļa finansējums; lt: sutelktinis finansavimas).
- Learn/academy is the ranking engine pre-licence: /en/learn/* and /en/regulation/* can be published BEFORE authorisation (educational content needs no licence), so the domain ages and accumulates authority during the Finantsinspektsioon application months; deal pages go live at launch into an already-ranking site.
- Comparison layer /en/compare/*: seedrs-republic-europe-alternative, crowdcube-alternative, funderbeam-alternative, seedrs-vs-crowdcube, us-platforms-vs-eu. Template: factual feature/fee/ownership-model table + 'why direct ownership differs' section. Legal review each page (comparative advertising rules).
- KIIS handling: each offer publishes KIIS as crawlable HTML (canonical) plus the official PDF download; offer pages carry required risk warnings server-side rendered so compliance text is part of the ranked content, not an overlay.
- Statistics page (/en/statistics) with quarterly-updated platform metrics and Estonian/Baltic market data — the designated link-earning asset for journalists (Crowdfund Insider, Eurocrowd, local media) and the ECSPR-transparency showcase.
- Clusters requiring localized versions at expansion (flagged now so templates are i18n-ready): category/country pages, raiser funnel, tax guides, 'is it safe' trust pages, and local competitor comparisons (fi: Invesdor/Springvest alternatives; lv: CrowdedHero alternative). Regulation explainers can stay EN-only longest — the audience reads English.

## Recommendations

- E-E-A-T for a licensed YMYL platform is the #1 ranking lever: footer on every page shows full legal name, ECSP licence number, and a DEEP link to the exact Finantsinspektsioon register entry (fi.ee) plus the ESMA crowdfunding register — verified live: both registers exist and are the trust anchors Google's raters are told to check. Add these the day the licence is granted; pre-licence, state 'authorisation application submitted to Finantsinspektsioon' honestly.
- Named humans everywhere: /en/about with founders, compliance officer and board (photos, bios, LinkedIn), and real author bylines with credentials on every /learn and /regulation article (Person schema + sameAs). Anonymous content on a financial YMYL site will not rank post-HCU.
- Structured data: Organization (with regulator sameAs links), BreadcrumbList, FAQPage on guides, Article with author on posts. Do NOT use Product/AggregateRating markup on investment offers — review-star rich results on financial products invite both Google penalties and ECSPR art 27 marketing-communication problems.
- Compliance-SEO integration: every indexable marketing/landing page is an ECSPR art 27 'marketing communication' — must be fair, clear, not misleading and consistent with the KIIS. Build a legal-review step into the content pipeline now; it also protects the licence application file.
- Launch content 6+ months BEFORE licence grant: the /learn, /regulation, /compare and statistics layers need no authorisation, and domain age + earned links should be compounding while Finantsinspektsioon processes the application (applications go via their portal since 18.03.2026).
- Digital PR shortlist (verified live and relevant): e-resident.gov.ee blog (already publishes 'build your startup investment portfolio with e-Residency' — pitch a guest feature), investinestonia.com, Eurocrowd membership + their ECSPR platform directory, Crowdfund Insider, and directory listings on crowdinform.com, thecrowdspace.com, investmentplatforms.eu, findcrowdfunding.com — these directories currently OWN the 'equity crowdfunding Estonia' SERP, so being listed in them is step one, outranking them is step two.
- Own the direct-ownership narrative: ESMA's 2 March 2026 nominee-structure Q&A makes 'nominee vs direct ownership' a live industry debate — publish the definitive explainer contrasting the client's Business-Register direct title (survives platform insolvency) with Crowdcube/Republic Europe nominee models. This is simultaneously the best SEO differentiator and the best conversion argument.
- Newsjack the ECSPR review cycle: the €5M→€12M offer-cap debate and each ESMA Q&A are recurring, low-competition, high-authority content moments; assign someone to publish within 48h of each regulatory development.
- Seedrs→Republic Europe brand confusion is a time-limited arbitrage: residual 'Seedrs' searches (rebrand July 2024, legal entity still Seedrs Limited) will decay over 2-3 years — ship the alternative/comparison pages in the first content batch.
- Bulletin-board pages: make secondary-market listings indexable with clear 'non-binding reference price' language — 'sell crowdfunding shares' queries have no good EU answer and Republic Europe is actively marketing secondary liquidity, confirming demand.
- Hreflang discipline: et/en pairs with x-default=en from day one, implemented in sitemaps not just head tags; when FI/LV/LT launch, add languages page-by-page (only translate pages that exist in that market's funnel) rather than machine-translating the whole site — partial, high-quality trees outperform full thin ones.
- Measurement guardrail: Baltic English search volumes are small in absolute terms — judge the program on qualified sign-ups and raiser leads per cluster, not raw traffic; the funded-deals programmatic layer and comparison pages will drive most conversions despite modest volume.
