FINANCIAL RISKS · ECSPR ART 19(1) READ BEFORE INVESTING

What can go wrong with an investment made here.

This page is not a disclaimer at the bottom of a marketing site. It is the list of ways you can lose money on this platform, written plainly, because you cannot judge an offer without it.

The short version. You can lose everything you invest. There is no market for these shares, so you may never be able to sell. No deposit guarantee or investor compensation scheme covers you. Most early-stage companies never pay their investors a return. Do not invest more than 10% of your net worth across all crowdfunding platforms, and only money you can afford to lose entirely.

The risks in detail

RiskWhat it means for you
Loss of capitalYou may lose all of the money you invest. Most early-stage companies return nothing to their investors, and a significant proportion cease trading altogether.
IlliquidityThere is no market for these shares. You may never be able to sell them. Our bulletin board lets investors advertise interest, but it does not guarantee a buyer exists.
DilutionLater funding rounds reduce your percentage holding. A stake of 2% today can become materially smaller after the company raises again, and you may have no right to participate.
Minority positionYou will not control the company. Decisions on strategy, further fundraising, and a sale of the business are made by others.
No dividendsMost project owners do not pay dividends, and none is promised. Any return depends on a future sale that may never happen.
No protection schemeNeither the deposit guarantee scheme (Directive 2014/49/EU) nor the investor compensation scheme (Directive 97/9/EC) applies to these investments.
Project owner insolvencyShareholders rank last if the company fails. In practice you would most likely receive nothing.
Nominee insolvencyShares are held for you by us, acting as nominee, and are kept separate from our own assets in our accounts and by contract. Even so, the risk that they would form part of our bankruptcy estate if we became insolvent cannot be excluded. See "The nominee structure" below.
ValuationThe price per share is set by the project owner. It is not independently verified, not approved by us, and not approved by any authority. You may pay more than the shares turn out to be worth.
InformationAfter you invest, the information you receive comes from the project owner. We pass it on; we do not verify its substance. Reporting obligations run for three years after a closing, then stop.
ConcentrationPutting a large share of your savings into one company, or into this asset class as a whole, concentrates all of the above into a single outcome.

The nominee structure

You are not entered on the project company's shareholder list. All shares acquired through this platform are held by us — AureviaFund OÜ, acting as nominee — in our own name and for your account. We are the registered shareholder in the Estonian Business Register. The structure is mandatory: direct shareholding is not available through this platform.

What you hold is a contractual claim against us, recorded in our nominee register. Economic rights — dividends, liquidation proceeds, proceeds of sale — belong to you in full. Voting rights are exercised only on your instruction; where you give none, we do not vote in respect of your holding. You pay nothing for the nominee service.

We operate the platform and act as nominee — the same legal person holds both roles. We manage that conflict by holding shares solely for your account, never on our own account, taking no economic interest in any offer, and never voting without your instruction. The structure has been disclosed to Finantsinspektsioon, and we have asked for its explicit position on it.

What protects you, and what does not

The four-day reflection period
You can withdraw any commitment within four calendar days, without a reason and at no cost. Your money is not released to the company before every investor's period has ended.

Your money sits with a licensed payment service provider in accounts separate from ours, and is returned in full if the offer does not close.

Required before your first investment, and renewed periodically. They help you judge whether this is appropriate for you — they do not make any individual investment safe.

Before you invest

Read the Key Investment Information Sheet for the specific offer. It contains the risks particular to that company — customer concentration, key-person dependency, funding needs, regulatory exposure — which this general page cannot cover. It is prepared by the project owner, who is responsible for it under ECSPR art 23.

We do not give investment advice and we do not assess whether a particular investment suits your personal circumstances. If you are unsure, speak to an independent adviser.
