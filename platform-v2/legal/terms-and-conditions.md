## 6. Investor classification, tests and limits

**6.1 Classification.** Every investor is classified as **non-sophisticated by default**. Sophisticated status is granted only on your written request, against the ECSPR Annex II criteria and supporting evidence, for a maximum of two years, and can be revoked or renounced at any time.

**6.2 What sophisticated status costs you.** Before we grant it we explain, and you confirm in a separate written statement, that you lose: the entry knowledge test, the loss-bearing simulation, the pre-investment warning above the threshold in clause 6.4, **and the four-day reflection period in clause 8**.

**6.3 Entry knowledge test and loss simulation.** Before your first investment as a non-sophisticated investor you must complete a knowledge test and a simulation of your ability to bear a loss of 10% of your net worth. If the result suggests our services may not be appropriate for you, we show a warning; you may still proceed after acknowledging it. **The knowledge test must be renewed every two years and the simulation every year — investing is blocked while either is missing or expired.**

**6.4 Threshold warning.** Where you invest more than the greater of **EUR 1,000** or **5% of your declared net worth**, we display a specific risk warning, obtain your express consent and take evidence that you understand the investment and its risks. We apply this test to each investment and to your cumulative position on the platform.

## 7. The nominee structure — please read carefully

**7.1 You are not entered on the project company's shareholder list.** All shares acquired through the platform are held by **us, AureviaFund OÜ, acting as nominee — in our own name and for your account**, under a nominee agreement you enter into before your first investment. We are the registered shareholder in the Estonian Business Register. There is no separate nominee company: the platform operator and the nominee are the same legal person.

**7.2 The nominee structure is mandatory.** Direct shareholding in a project company is not available through the platform.

**7.3 What you hold.** Your entitlement is a **contractual claim against the nominee**, recorded in the nominee register and visible in your account. Specifically:

- **economic rights** — dividends, liquidation proceeds and proceeds of sale — belong to you in full and are paid to you through the PSP;
- **voting rights** are exercised by the nominee **only on your instruction**; where you give no instruction, the nominee does not vote in respect of your holding;
- **information rights** received by the nominee are passed on to you through the platform;
- **you pay nothing** for the nominee service (clause 9).

**7.4 Segregation and residual risk.** Shares held for your account are kept separate from the nominee's own assets in its accounts and by contract. **Nevertheless, the risk that those shares would form part of the nominee's bankruptcy estate if the nominee became insolvent cannot be excluded.** This risk is described in the risk factors of every KIIS. Please read it before investing.

**7.5 If we cease operating.** The transfer procedure in the nominee agreement and our business continuity plan applies: holdings are transferred either to investors themselves or to a replacement nominee. We notify you and give you a statement of your holdings.

**7.6 Conflicts — we hold both roles.** We operate the platform *and* act as nominee. We manage the conflict this creates by: holding shares solely for your account and never on our own account; taking no economic interest in any offer; never voting without your instruction; keeping the nominee register separate from our own assets in our accounts; and taking no fee from you for the nominee function. The full policy is published on the platform. We have disclosed this structure to Finantsinspektsioon and sought its explicit position on it.

**7.7 Asset safekeeping.** Because we hold the shares and maintain the record of your entitlement, we treat this as an **asset safekeeping service under ECSPR art 10(1)** and give you the information that article requires: the nature and terms of the service, the applicable Estonian law (Commercial Code, Law of Obligations Act), and the fact that the service is provided **by us directly, not by a third party**.

## 8. Conflicts of interest

**8.1** ECSPR art 8 requires us to identify, prevent, manage and disclose conflicts of interest. Three arise from the way this platform is built, and you should know about them before you invest.

**8.2** **We appear on the shareholder register of every company we list.** The nominee arrangement in section 7 means AureviaFund OÜ operates the platform and is at the same time the registered holder of the shares. That is a structural conflict, not an incidental one. We manage it by keeping the nominee register and the shares held under it separate from our own assets, by recording every entitlement against the investor it belongs to, and by never exercising a vote against that investor's instruction.

**8.3** **We are paid only when a round succeeds.** Our success fee is 6% of the amount raised, so we earn nothing on an offer that fails. That gives us a financial interest in offers closing — including offers that perhaps should not. We manage it by publishing our admission criteria in full and applying them before any commercial discussion, by having the completeness of each Key Investment Information Sheet checked by two reviewers independently, and by publishing our acceptance rate once a year whatever it says.

**8.4** **Neither we nor anyone connected to us may use this platform.** Under ECSPR art 8(1) we do not invest in offers on our own platform. Under art 8(2) our shareholders holding 20% or more, our directors, our employees, and anyone connected to them by control or close family ties cannot be a project owner here. There is no exception and no process for granting one.

**8.5** We maintain a register of conflicts of interest. Where a conflict cannot be prevented it is escalated to compliance and disclosed to the affected client before they act. If you believe a conflict has affected how you were treated, that is a complaint and it costs you nothing — see section 16.

## 9. Your right to withdraw — the reflection period

> **You have four calendar days.**

**9.1** As a non-sophisticated investor you may withdraw your investment offer **at any time during four calendar days from the moment you make it, without giving a reason and without incurring any fee or penalty**.

**9.2** The period runs in calendar days, including weekends and public holidays, from the timestamp recorded when you commit. We show you the exact end date and time, remind you before it expires, and display the remaining time in your account.

**9.3** To withdraw, use the withdrawal control in your account or the link in your confirmation email, or write to us by any clear means. We will not ask your reason and will not try to talk you out of it.

**9.4** Your money is not released to the project owner before every participating investor's reflection period has ended. On withdrawal your funds are returned in full, without deduction, to the account they came from, within **5 business days**.

**9.5** Sophisticated investors do not have this right. We tell you this when sophisticated status is granted and again at the point of investment.

## 11. How investing works

**11.1** You read the KIIS, which is prepared and owned by the project owner. You commit an amount (minimum EUR 100). We display any warnings required by clause 6. Your reflection period starts.

**11.2** You transfer funds by SEPA bank transfer or card payment to a **segregated client account operated by the PSP**. Funds are attributed to you and to the specific offer.

**10.3 We will never ask you to transfer money to a project owner directly, or to any account other than the one shown on the platform.** Any such request is fraud — do not pay, and report it to us immediately.

**11.4** If the offer does not reach its minimum target, or is cancelled or suspended and not resumed, **all funds are returned in full, without deduction**.

**11.5** On a successful closing, funds are released, the nominee is entered as shareholder and your entitlement is recorded in the nominee register. You receive a confirmation.
