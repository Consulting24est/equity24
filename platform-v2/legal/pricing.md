**No hidden margin.** We take no spread on your money, no carry on your gains, no exit fee, and no rebate from any project owner. Our revenue comes only from the fees on this page, paid by companies.

## Companies raising capital

| Fee | Amount | When |
|---|---|---|
| **Application fee** | **EUR 1,000** | Payable when you apply. Covers screening and due diligence. Non-refundable |
| **Listing fee** | **EUR 1,000** | Charged if we accept you, before the offer opens. Non-refundable |
| **Success fee** | **6% of funds raised** | Only on a successful closing |
| **Post-closing reporting administration** | **EUR 500 per year for 3 years** | Annually after a successful closing |

**If the round does not close, you pay nothing beyond the application fee and the listing fee.** No success fee, no reporting fee.

### What is included

Due diligence and screening · offer page and data room · KIIS template and written guidance · the completeness check on your KIIS · investor onboarding, classification, appropriateness testing and warnings · payment handling through a licensed payment service provider with segregated client accounts · the nominee structure and register, so your cap table gains one line rather than dozens · your project community channel · investor reporting distribution for three years · bulletin board access for your investors.

### What is not included

Your own legal, tax and audit costs · preparing the KIIS itself, which is your document and your responsibility under ECSPR art 23 · notary or registry fees where your national law requires them · your own marketing beyond the platform.

## Why we charge this way

Charging investors would take money out of the investment itself and give us a reason to prefer volume over quality. Charging the company, only on success, ties what we earn to whether the raise actually worked. The reporting fee covers three years of distributing your reports to investors — an obligation that continues long after the round closes, and one that platforms which ignore it tend to fund by quietly charging investors instead.

4. **"Effective cost of capital"** figures are arithmetic on the fees only; they exclude the company's own legal and audit costs. Keep the qualifier.
