3. What we do — and what we do not do

3.1 We do: operate an internet-based platform that brings together investors and project owners; carry out the due diligence on project owners required by ECSPR art 5; check that each KIIS is complete, correct and clear; classify investors and administer the tests and warnings required by ECSPR arts 19–22; maintain the nominee register; and operate a bulletin board under art 25.

3.2 We do not:

  • give investment advice or personal recommendations, or assess whether an investment is suitable for your personal circumstances beyond the appropriateness assessment the regulation requires;
  • approve, endorse, recommend or guarantee any offer. Listing an offer means only that our published admission criteria were met and the KIIS passed our completeness check;
  • hold your money. Funds are held by the PSP in segregated client accounts;
  • operate a trading venue. We do not match orders, execute transactions or set prices;
  • guarantee any return, the success of any offer, or that you will find a buyer for your shares.

3.3 No offer on the platform has been approved or reviewed by Finantsinspektsioon or any other competent authority. Our review of a KIIS does not transfer the project owner's responsibility for it.

4. Risk warning

Investing in crowdfunding projects is risky and may result in the loss of all of the capital invested. Your investment is not covered by a deposit guarantee scheme (Directive 2014/49/EU) or an investor compensation scheme (Directive 97/9/EC). You may not receive any return on your investment and you may not be able to sell the instruments when you wish. Do not invest more than you can afford to lose.

You should not invest more than 10% of your net worth in crowdfunding projects across all platforms.

6. Investor classification, tests and limits

6.1 Classification. Every investor is classified as non-sophisticated by default. Sophisticated status is granted only on your written request, against the ECSPR Annex II criteria and supporting evidence, for a maximum of two years, and can be revoked or renounced at any time.

6.2 What sophisticated status costs you. Before we grant it we explain, and you confirm in a separate written statement, that you lose: the entry knowledge test, the loss-bearing simulation, the pre-investment warning above the threshold in clause 6.4, and the four-day reflection period in clause 8.

6.3 Entry knowledge test and loss simulation. Before your first investment as a non-sophisticated investor you must complete a knowledge test and a simulation of your ability to bear a loss of 10% of your net worth. If the result suggests our services may not be appropriate for you, we show a warning; you may still proceed after acknowledging it. The knowledge test must be renewed every two years and the simulation every year — investing is blocked while either is missing or expired.

6.4 Threshold warning. Where you invest more than the greater of EUR 1,000 or 5% of your declared net worth, we display a specific risk warning, obtain your express consent and take evidence that you understand the investment and its risks. We apply this test to each investment and to your cumulative position on the platform.

7. The nominee structure — please read carefully

7.1 You are not entered on the project company's shareholder list. All shares acquired through the platform are held by us, AureviaFund OÜ, acting as nominee — in our own name and for your account, under a nominee agreement you enter into before your first investment. We are the registered shareholder in the Estonian Business Register. There is no separate nominee company: the platform operator and the nominee are the same legal person.

7.2 The nominee structure is mandatory. Direct shareholding in a project company is not available through the platform.

7.3 What you hold. Your entitlement is a contractual claim against the nominee, recorded in the nominee register and visible in your account. Specifically:

  • economic rights — dividends, liquidation proceeds and proceeds of sale — belong to you in full and are paid to you through the PSP;
  • voting rights are exercised by the nominee only on your instruction; where you give no instruction, the nominee does not vote in respect of your holding;
  • information rights received by the nominee are passed on to you through the platform;
  • you pay nothing for the nominee service (clause 9).

7.4 Segregation and residual risk. Shares held for your account are kept separate from the nominee's own assets in its accounts and by contract. Nevertheless, the risk that those shares would form part of the nominee's bankruptcy estate if the nominee became insolvent cannot be excluded. This risk is described in the risk factors of every KIIS. Please read it before investing.

7.5 If we cease operating. The transfer procedure in the nominee agreement and our business continuity plan applies: holdings are transferred either to investors themselves or to a replacement nominee. We notify you and give you a statement of your holdings.

7.6 Conflicts — we hold both roles. We operate the platform and act as nominee. We manage the conflict this creates by: holding shares solely for your account and never on our own account; taking no economic interest in any offer; never voting without your instruction; keeping the nominee register separate from our own assets in our accounts; and taking no fee from you for the nominee function. The full policy is published on the platform. We have disclosed this structure to Finantsinspektsioon and sought its explicit position on it.

7.7 Asset safekeeping. Because we hold the shares and maintain the record of your entitlement, we treat this as an asset safekeeping service under ECSPR art 10(1) and give you the information that article requires: the nature and terms of the service, the applicable Estonian law (Commercial Code, Law of Obligations Act), and the fact that the service is provided by us directly, not by a third party.

8. Your right to withdraw — the reflection period

You have four calendar days.

8.1 As a non-sophisticated investor you may withdraw your investment offer at any time during four calendar days from the moment you make it, without giving a reason and without incurring any fee or penalty.

8.2 The period runs in calendar days, including weekends and public holidays, from the timestamp recorded when you commit. We show you the exact end date and time, remind you before it expires, and display the remaining time in your account.

8.3 To withdraw, use the withdrawal control in your account or the link in your confirmation email, or write to us by any clear means. We will not ask your reason and will not try to talk you out of it.

8.4 Your money is not released to the project owner before every participating investor's reflection period has ended. On withdrawal your funds are returned in full, without deduction, to the account they came from, within 5 business days.

8.5 Sophisticated investors do not have this right. We tell you this when sophisticated status is granted and again at the point of investment.

9. Fees

9.1 Investors pay nothing. No account fee, investment fee, nominee register fee, withdrawal fee, inactivity fee, exit fee or bulletin board fee. This is permanent, not promotional. Payment costs are borne by us.

9.2 Project owners pay: a listing fee, a success fee on funds raised, and a post-closing reporting administration fee. The current schedule is published at equity24.io/pricing and reproduced in each KIIS.

9.3 We take no other benefit. We do not receive or pay any commission, discount or non-monetary benefit that would induce anyone to steer investors towards a particular offer. Any interest we hold in a project owner is disclosed on the offer page and in the KIIS.

9.4 If we ever introduce a fee for investors we will give at least 30 days' notice, and you may close your account before it takes effect. Fees can never be applied retroactively to investments already made.

10. How investing works

10.1 You read the KIIS, which is prepared and owned by the project owner. You commit an amount (minimum EUR 100). We display any warnings required by clause 6. Your reflection period starts.

10.2 You transfer funds by SEPA bank transfer or card payment to a segregated client account operated by the PSP. Funds are attributed to you and to the specific offer.

10.3 We will never ask you to transfer money to a project owner directly, or to any account other than the one shown on the platform. Any such request is fraud — do not pay, and report it to us immediately.

10.4 If the offer does not reach its minimum target, or is cancelled or suspended and not resumed, all funds are returned in full, without deduction.

10.5 On a successful closing, funds are released, the nominee is entered as shareholder and your entitlement is recorded in the nominee register. You receive a confirmation.

11. Suspension and cancellation of offers

We may suspend or cancel an offer at any time, including where the KIIS turns out to be incomplete, incorrect or unclear and is not corrected; where the project owner's circumstances change materially; where fraud is suspected; where the EUR 5,000,000 twelve-month limit would be exceeded; or where a supervisory authority requires it. We tell affected investors within 24 hours and arrange the return of funds. We are not liable to a project owner for a lawful suspension or cancellation.

12. Selling later — the bulletin board

12.1 The bulletin board lets investors advertise buying and selling interest in shares of companies funded through the platform.

12.2 It is not an exchange. We do not bring buying and selling interests together, match orders, execute transactions, or set or guarantee any price. Any price shown is the poster's own non-binding indication and may not reflect achievable value.

12.3 The parties negotiate and agree between themselves, outside our protocols. Where both instruct us, we record the agreed transfer in the nominee register. A seller must make the relevant KIIS available; a non-sophisticated buyer receives the information and risk warning the regulation requires.

12.4 There may be no buyer when you want to sell. Treat your investment as one you may have to hold indefinitely.
