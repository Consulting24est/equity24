2. The short version

We collect what we need to verify who you are, to judge whether our service is appropriate for you, to execute and record your investments, and to meet obligations that financial regulation imposes on us. We do not sell your data. We keep investment records for at least five years because the law requires it — that obligation overrides a deletion request. Identity documents are checked by our verification provider and we keep the decision, not the documents.

3. What we collect

3.1 You give us: name, personal identification code or date of birth, nationality, residential address, email, phone; for companies, registry data and details of representatives and beneficial owners; your answers to the knowledge test; the income, asset and liability ranges used for the loss-bearing simulation; your investment amounts and instructions; complaints and correspondence; community posts.

3.2 We generate or receive about you: verification results from our provider; sanctions and politically-exposed-person screening results; your investor classification; the warnings shown to you and the consents you gave, with timestamps and a hash of what was on screen; reflection-period start, end and any withdrawal; your holdings in the nominee register; payment confirmations from the payment service provider.

3.3 Collected automatically: IP address, device and browser, session and login events, pages viewed and actions taken, and security logs.

3.4 Data minimisation in practice. Financial information for the loss simulation is collected in ranges, not exact amounts, because a range is enough for the calculation. We do not retain copies of identity documents where our verification provider retains them under contract.

4.1 Criminal-record data. ECSPR art 5 requires us to check that project owners, their directors and beneficial owners have no relevant convictions. This is special-category-adjacent processing under GDPR art 10, permitted only where authorised by Union or member-state law. We process it strictly for that check, keep it in a restricted store accessible to named individuals only, and delete it at the end of the retention period without exception. ⚠️ Counsel must confirm the national legal basis before the first project owner is onboarded.

4.2 What we never do. We do not use the financial information collected for the loss simulation for marketing, segmentation or pricing. It is used for the statutory assessment and retained as a record — nothing else.

4.3 Automated decisions. Investor classification and the investment barriers are rule-based, not profiling: fixed thresholds applied to what you declared. We explain any outcome and a person will review it on request. We do not make decisions producing legal effects based on automated profiling.

8. Your rights

You may ask us to:

  • give you access to your personal data and a copy of it;
  • correct inaccurate data — much of it you can correct yourself in your account;
  • delete data — but see the note below;
  • restrict processing while a dispute about accuracy or legitimate interest is resolved;
  • port data you gave us, in a machine-readable format;
  • object to processing based on legitimate interest;
  • withdraw consent to marketing at any time, with immediate effect and no consequence for your account.

About deletion. Investment, assessment and verification records cannot generally be deleted during the statutory retention period — ECSPR art 26 and anti-money-laundering law override the right to erasure. We will explain what we can delete and what we must keep, and why.

How to exercise them. Write to info@equity24.io. We verify your identity first, and answer within one month, extendable by two months for complex requests, which we would tell you about. Exercising your rights is free unless a request is manifestly unfounded or excessive.

Complaints. You can complain to the Estonian Data Protection Inspectorate (Tatari 39, Tallinn, info@aki.ee, aki.ee) or to the authority in your country of residence, and you can go to court.

9. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest (AES-256). Access is role-based on a least-privilege basis, reviewed twice a year and revoked the day someone leaves. Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for our staff. Restricted data — identity documents, financial declarations, criminal-record data — is separately encrypted and every access is logged. Production data is never used in test environments. We run vulnerability scans quarterly and an external penetration test annually.

Personal data breaches. We assess within 24 hours, notify the Data Protection Inspectorate within 72 hours where there is a risk to your rights, and tell you directly without undue delay where the risk is high.
