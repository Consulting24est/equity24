# Brand naming + messaging — Estonian equity crowdfunding platform

## 10 candidates

**A. Estonian-rooted**

1. **Osalus** ("shareholding/participation") — semantically exact for equity; the *osa* root also means "share/part" in Finnish, so it travels north. Search shows no fintech collision. Risk: generic ET word → weak standalone trademark in class 36; register as composite (word + logo) and hold osalus.com/.eu/.ee. .com looks obtainable; verify .ee.
2. **Kasvu** ("growth" in both ET and FI) — warm, pan-Finnic. Risk: **Kasvu Therapeutics** (Helsinki) and **Kasvu Group** (FI consultancy) exist; different classes but crowded in the priority expansion market. Medium risk.
3. **Omaosa** ("own share") — friendly, ownable. Risk: sounds diminutive in EN ("oma-osa"); low institutional weight.
4. **Osak** — clipped, modern. Risk: *osak* means fund unit in ET usage — could imply collective investment (AIFMD adjacency); avoid.

**B. Latin / pan-European**

5. **Partem** (Latin "a share") — sounds like a licensed firm; clean search results. Risk: phonetic proximity to Partech Partners (VC); low-medium.
6. **Auctum** (Latin "grown/increased") — institutional. Risk: Auctus crypto project residue; check EUIPO.
7. **Socium** (Latin "partner") — credible. Risk: several "Socium" HR/consulting firms exist; medium.

**C. Descriptive-compound**

8. **Sharefront** — clear in EN. Risk: opaque in ET/LV/LT; "front" can read aggressive.
9. **Ownlist** — describes the curated listing model. Risk: sounds SaaS, not financial institution.
10. **Silda / Osasild** (*sild* = bridge) — nice metaphor. Risk: **crowded** — Silta Finance (Finnish DeFi; *silta* = bridge in FI) and Sila (fintech API, Goldman-acquired). Avoid.

## Top 3 and recommendation

1. **Osalus** 2. **Partem** 3. **Kasvu**

**Recommended working name: Osalus** (entity: Osalus Capital OÜ; domain plan: osalus.com primary, osalus.eu/.ee defensive). It says exactly what the investor gets, is pronounceable in all five target languages, carries no crypto/lending baggage, and searches clean. Mitigate the generic-word trademark weakness with a figurative EU trademark filing before public launch.

## Messaging kit — Osalus

**Tagline**
- EN: *Real companies. Real shares. Fully digital.*
- ET: *Päris ettevõtted. Päris osalus. Täisdigitaalne.*

**Positioning line**
Osalus is an equity crowdfunding platform (ECSPR licence application pending with Finantsinspektsioon) where investors subscribe directly to shares in established Estonian companies — ownership recorded in the Estonian Business Register, not on our servers, and no investor fees of any kind.

**Investor value props**
- **Register-backed ownership.** Your shares are entered in the Estonian Business Register; a platform-run cap-table is evidence, not custody. Your holding exists independently of Osalus.
- **EUR 0 investor fees.** No account, subscription, custody or exit fees — project owners pay for the service.
- **Vetted, operating businesses only.** Every issuer has 3+ years of trading history, EUR 0.5–3M revenue and at least EUR 10,000 paid-in capital. You can withdraw any commitment free of charge within 4 calendar days.

**Founder / project-owner value props**
- **Growth equity without giving up control.** Raise EUR 150–800k for 5–15% of your company from investors who choose you — not a bank covenant, not a VC board seat.
- **Fully digital closing.** With notary-waiving articles, subscriptions and later transfers are signed digitally — weeks, not months.
- **One transparent price.** EUR 1,000 listing fee + 6% only on a successful raise; later access to Finnish, Latvian and Lithuanian investors under the same EU licence.

**Tone-of-voice rules (regulated-calm)**
1. Never promise or project returns; every benefit statement is paired with its condition or risk (ECSPR art 27 — marketing must be fair, clear, not misleading).
2. Numbers over adjectives: "38 investors, EUR 412,000 subscribed", never "massively oversubscribed".
3. The capital-loss risk warning is designed-in on every page — same type size as body copy, never collapsed or greyed out.
4. No urgency mechanics: no countdown timers, scarcity banners, FOMO copy; the 4-day reflection period is presented as a feature.
5. Plain-language B1 register in ET and EN; no anglicised startup slang, exclamation marks or emojis. Sentence case throughout.
6. Say "licence application pending" until granted — never imply supervision that doesn't yet exist.
